# Voltara — launch kit

## Headline
Energy monitoring that fits in one container

## Subhead
A single-process dashboard for operators who need site-level answers in under 60 seconds, not a stack of services.

## Launch post
Voltara ships as a single Docker container with an embedded SQLite database—no external services, no environment variables, no setup beyond a volume mount. Regional operators assess 15–40 substation sites through live-derived KPIs, status-driven workflows, and full CRUD across sites, meters, readings, tariffs, and alerts. CSV import handles 10,000 readings in under 30 seconds, and the entire deployment takes one docker run command behind your existing VPN.
